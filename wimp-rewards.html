What WIMP Rewards are

WIMP Rewards are promotional points provided by Wimps. They are not Ghanaian cedi, electronic money, cryptocurrency, or an investment. They cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to other users. They can only be used for eligible Wimps products and discounts, subject to the Wimps Rewards Terms.

Earn WIMP

Eligible completed data-bundle purchases can earn the reward configured by Wimps. Failed, cancelled, reversed, and refunded orders do not earn points.

Spend WIMP

Points can only reduce the price of eligible Wimps products. The available balance and final discount are always calculated by the server.
